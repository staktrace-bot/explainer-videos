# Explainer Videos

Short, kid-friendly explainer videos. Watch at https://staktrace-bot.github.io/explainer-videos/

- **What Is a Court?** (`videos/what-is-a-court.mp4`, ~108s) — judge, jury, lawyers, witnesses, fairness, and why we have courts. Captions: `.vtt`; script: `videos/what-is-a-court-script.txt`.
- **How to Read a Clock** (`videos/how-to-read-a-clock.mp4`, ~100s, ages 5-8) — hour and minute hands, o'clock, counting by fives, half past, quarter past/to, and practice. Captions: `.vtt`; script: `videos/how-to-read-a-clock-script.txt`.
- **How Can a Stool Hold Your Weight?** (`videos/how-stools-hold-your-weight.mp4`, ~93s, ages 5-8) — gravity and weight, legs sharing the load, the floor pushing back, stability and tipping, compression and cross braces, and a paper-cup stool activity. Captions: `.vtt`; script: `videos/how-stools-hold-your-weight-script.txt`.
- **Interesting Things About 100** (`videos/interesting-things-about-100.mp4`, ~113s, ages 5-8) — ten tens, 100 cents, 100%, boiling at 100°C, centimetre/century, 100th birthday, 10×10 grid, and the centipede myth. Captions: `.vtt`; script: `videos/interesting-things-about-100-script.txt`.
- **Interesting Things About 1,000** (`videos/interesting-things-about-1000.mp4`, ~113s, ages 5-8) — ten hundreds, 10×10×10 cube, kilo/milli, millennium, millipede myth, counting to 1,000, Roman numeral M. Captions: `.vtt`; script: `videos/interesting-things-about-1000-script.txt`.

Made with Pillow-drawn animation and ffmpeg. Narration: Piper TTS (en_US-amy) for the court video; Kokoro TTS (af_heart voice) for the clock, stool, 100, and 1,000 videos.
