How to Read a Clock — narration script

[How to Read a Clock]
Hi friends! Today we're going to learn how to read a clock. It's easier than you think!

[The Numbers 1 to 12]
A clock has the numbers one to twelve, all the way around in a circle. Twelve is at the very top.

[Two Hands]
A clock has two hands. The short hand is the hour hand. The long hand is the minute hand.

[O'Clock]
When the long minute hand points straight up at the twelve, it's something o'clock! The short hand points at seven, so it's seven o'clock.

[Counting by Fives]
The minute hand counts by fives! Each number means five more minutes. Five, ten, fifteen, twenty, twenty-five, thirty, thirty-five, forty, forty-five, fifty, fifty-five, sixty!

[Half Past]
When the minute hand points down at the six, it's half past. That's thirty minutes. This clock says half past two, or two thirty.

[Quarter Past]
When the minute hand points at the three, it's quarter past. That's fifteen minutes. Here it's quarter past four.

[Quarter To]
When the minute hand points at the nine, it's quarter to. Only fifteen minutes left until the next hour! This is quarter to five.

[Practice Time!]
Your turn! Look at the clock. What time is it? Think about it.

[Practice Time!]
It's eight o'clock! The long hand is on the twelve, and the short hand is on the eight. Great job!

[One More!]
Here's another one. Where is the long hand pointing? What time is it?

[One More!]
It's half past six! The long hand is on the six, and the short hand is between six and seven. You did it!

[You Can Read a Clock!]
Now you know the hour hand, the minute hand, o'clock, half past, and quarter past and quarter to. Keep practicing, clock reader!
