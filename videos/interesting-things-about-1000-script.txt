Interesting Things About 1,000 — narration script

[Interesting Things About 1,000!]
Hi friends! Remember one hundred? Today we're meeting an even BIGGER number: one thousand! We write it with a one and three zeros. Let's discover what makes it so special!

[Ten Hundreds]
Let's count by hundreds! One hundred, two hundred, three hundred, four hundred, five hundred, six hundred, seven hundred, eight hundred, nine hundred, one thousand! Ten hundreds make one thousand.

[A Cube of 1,000]
Build a cube with ten little blocks across, ten blocks back, and ten layers high. Ten times ten times ten. That's one thousand tiny blocks!

[Kilo Means 1,000]
Words that start with kilo mean one thousand. A kilometre is one thousand metres. That's a long walk! And a kilogram is one thousand grams, about as heavy as a big bottle of water.

[Milli Means Tiny Pieces]
Milli goes the other way. Chop a metre into one thousand teeny pieces, and each one is a millimetre. And one second is one thousand milliseconds. Blink! That took a few hundred of them.

[A Millennium]
A thousand years has a special name: a millennium! From the year one thousand to the year two thousand was one whole millennium. That's ten centuries!

[Millipede Myth!]
Millipede means thousand feet. But most millipedes have way fewer than a thousand legs! Scientists did find one tiny millipede with over thirteen hundred legs. Wow!

[Counting to 1,000]
If you counted to one thousand, saying one number every second, it would take about seventeen minutes! And one thousand days is almost three whole years.

[The Letter M]
Long ago, the Romans wrote numbers with letters. To them, one thousand was the letter M. So M is for a thousand!

[Ten Hundreds Make 1,000!]
So remember: ten hundreds, a ten by ten by ten cube, kilometres, millimetres, a millennium, and the letter M. One thousand is amazing. Great job, number explorers!
