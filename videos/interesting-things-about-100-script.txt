Interesting Things About 100 — narration script

[Interesting Things About 100!]
Hi friends! Today we're going to explore a big, special number: one hundred! We use one hundred all the time, at school, with money, and when we play. Let's find out why it's so cool!

[Ten Groups of Ten]
Count with me by tens! Ten, twenty, thirty, forty, fifty, sixty, seventy, eighty, ninety, one hundred! See? Ten groups of ten make one hundred. That's how we get there!

[One Hundred Cents]
Look at money! One hundred cents make one dollar. If you save one hundred pennies, or four shiny quarters, you have a whole dollar. Cha-ching!

[One Hundred Percent!]
Have you heard people say one hundred percent? One hundred percent means all of something. If you eat one hundred percent of your cookies, you ate every single one. None left!

[Water Boils at 100!]
Here's a hot fact! Water boils at one hundred degrees Celsius. When a kettle hits one hundred, steam comes whooshing out. Don't touch! Ask a grown-up to show you safely.

[Centi Means One Hundredth]
Words with centi are about one hundred. A centimetre is one hundredth of a metre — super tiny! And a century is one hundred years. Wow, that's a long, long time.

[A Hundredth Birthday!]
Imagine living one hundred years. That's a century-old birthday party! People blow out one hundred candles on a giant cake. What a celebration!

[A Ten by Ten Square]
Draw a square with ten little boxes across and ten little boxes down. Fill them in and count: one, two, three... all the way to one hundred little squares! Neat, right?

[Centipede Myth!]
People say a centipede has one hundred legs. But that's a myth! Centipedes can have lots and lots of legs, but not always exactly one hundred. Surprise!

[You Can Count to 100!]
So remember: ten tens, one hundred cents, one hundred percent, boiling water, centuries, and a ten-by-ten grid. You can count to one hundred. Great job, number explorers!
